# Fixtures

Everything in this folder is **synthetic data generated by the team**. It is not part of the
Factored Datathon dataset and contains no real people: names, documents, emails and phone
numbers are invented (`example.com`, `Cliente Ficticio`, `FIX` identifiers).

Fixtures are versioned on purpose (`.gitignore` re-includes `fixtures/**/*.csv`) so tests are
reproducible without access to the S3 bucket.

## `late_arrival_v2/`

Exercises the incremental update of silver and gold with the same layout as the S3 bucket
(`data/...`).
Used by [`tests/test_late_arrival.py`](../tests/test_late_arrival.py).

| Stage | Files | Content |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `base/` | `branches.csv`, `customers.csv`, `products.csv`, `transactions/.../day=16/part-0.csv` | One branch, two customers, two products and two transactions on 2026-06-16 (`TRX-FIX-0001`, `TRX-FIX-0002`) |
| `late/` | `transactions/year=2024/month=01/day=10/part-late.csv` | **Late partition**: two transactions for 2024-01-10, far older than the watermark (2026-06-16). It also carries a **new column** `device_fingerprint` (additive schema change). `TRX-FIX-0003` has no `amount_usd`, so silver fills it with the COP booking rate |
| `late/` | `transactions/year=2026/month=06/day=16/part-1.csv` | **Duplicate**: `TRX-FIX-0002` re-delivered in another file with a corrected amount (7,000 ARS instead of 3,500); the later ingestion must win |

Expected silver result after loading `base/` and then `late/`:

- `transactions` is updated incrementally, reprocessing only `2024-01` (late partition) and
  `2026-06` (trailing window); other months are untouched.
- 4 transactions, `TRX-FIX-0002` with amount 7,000 ARS, the duplicate counted once.
- The 2 late rows are counted as older than the 7-day window and still applied.
- `device_fingerprint` is kept as text; `TRX-FIX-0003.amount_usd` = 800,000 / 4,000 = 200.00.

Expected gold result after `late/`:

- `transaction_features` is recomputed: `TRX-FIX-0001` (customer 1, 2026-06-16) goes from no
  history to 1 prior transaction (the late 2024-01-10 one) and ~888 days of observed tenure.
- `customer_360` counts 2 transactions for customer 1 instead of 1.
- The corrected duplicate (`TRX-FIX-0002`, USD 20.00) is the one gold sees.
